# 01 — Split Showcase

**Best for:** a design agency, creative studio, or premium service launch.

## Desktop composition

- Canvas: 1440 × 1024px; 64px page margin.
- Two equal 656px panels separated at the centre.
- Left: full-bleed image, subtle corner pattern, supporting copy, centred white CTA.
- Right: teal gradient with a texture overlay, 56px white headline, and a 2 × 2 feature grid.

## Content contract

- Headline: 3 lines maximum.
- Feature grid: exactly four concise benefits, numbered `01`–`04`.
- CTA: one 1–3 word action label.

## Responsive behavior

Stack image content first and proof content second below 900px. Keep the feature grid at two columns until 520px, then use one column.
