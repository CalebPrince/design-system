# 02 — Editorial Metrics

**Best for:** fashion, beauty, photography, or a brand with a strong visual point of view.

## Desktop composition

- Soft off-white canvas with a tall image anchored to the left edge.
- 82px serif headline overlaps the image and open canvas.
- Bottom-left introduction and black pill CTA.
- A wide translucent statistics card overlaps the right half; place its circular text accent above the card.

## Content contract

- Headline: 4–6 words; use a single italic/serif emphasis phrase.
- Metrics: four evidence points with compact labels.
- Avoid long body copy; this template is image-led.

## Responsive behavior

Place headline after the image, then body/CTA, then statistics. Turn four metrics into a two-column grid on tablet and one column on narrow mobile.
