# 03 — Creative Collection

**Best for:** a portfolio, product collection, or creative campaign.

## Desktop composition

- Editorial headline at upper left with a prominent vertical product/device image.
- Three numbered supporting content blocks form a visual sequence.
- Preserve generous empty space around the hero rather than adding navigation-heavy chrome.

## Content contract

- Headline: 3–5 words.
- Main image: one clear product or portfolio visual.
- Supporting blocks: three short descriptions, each tied to a numbered point.

## Responsive behavior

Put the hero image directly below the headline. Convert the supporting blocks to a horizontal scroll list or stacked cards; retain number order.
