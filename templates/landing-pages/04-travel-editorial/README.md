# 04 — Travel Editorial

**Best for:** travel, hospitality, destination, or experience-led marketing.

## Desktop composition

- Warm photographic background with a full-page landscape crop.
- A small editorial emblem provides a focal detail near the image edge.
- Text column on the right: 56px serif headline, supporting copy, and a dark CTA.
- Supporting number markers and concise labels add social proof without competing with the destination image.

## Content contract

- Headline: up to 2 lines.
- Use one landscape image with room for text contrast.
- Keep destination metrics to 2–4 simple, comparable values.

## Responsive behavior

Apply a contrast overlay to the image and place the copy in a solid or translucent surface below 768px. Never place text over an uncontrolled photographic focal area.
