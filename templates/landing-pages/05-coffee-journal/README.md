# 05 — Coffee Journal

**Best for:** a food, hospitality, editorial, or artisan-brand landing page.

## Desktop composition

- Clean white canvas with oversized, text-first serif storytelling.
- Background image occupies the full composition as a quiet visual texture.
- Two dark vertical cards on the right combine large metrics, a product image, and low-contrast supporting text.
- A black pill CTA anchors the story panel.

## Content contract

- Headline: 3 short lines, ideally 5–8 words in total.
- Cards: two featured products, locations, stories, or proof points.
- Use a tightly art-directed still-life image; its crop is part of the composition.

## Responsive behavior

Move the background image into a top media block and stack the cards after the CTA. Ensure the low-contrast card copy meets accessibility contrast requirements before publishing.
