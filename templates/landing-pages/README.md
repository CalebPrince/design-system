# Landing Page Templates

Five landing-page templates adapted from the supplied Figma reference. They share a 1440px desktop reference canvas, 64px outer margins, prominent editorial type, high-impact imagery, and pill-shaped primary actions. They are intentionally separate visual systems: do not merge their tokens into the global product system without brand review.

| Template | Visual direction | Primary layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-split-showcase` | Bold teal split-screen | Image panel + numbered proof panel |
| `02-editorial-metrics` | Fashion/editorial | Offset image, oversized headline, glass stats |
| `03-creative-collection` | Technology gallery | Editorial title + product image cards |
| `04-travel-editorial` | Warm landscape story | Editorial copy + destination photo/metrics |
| `05-coffee-journal` | Monochrome hospitality | Text-first hero + stacked image cards |

Use licensed, project-owned replacement images. The source Figma image links are temporary and are not committed to this repository.
